Project Overview

For my data science project, I am analyzing Drake’s music career and commercial longevity compared to his peers in the hip-hop industry. Using data scraped from Billboard’s official chart history, this page explores how Drake’s output compares to fellow dominant rap artists from 2009 through 2026.
